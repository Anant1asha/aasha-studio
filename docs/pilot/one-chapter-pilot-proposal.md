# One-Chapter Learning Pilot Proposal

**Status:** Draft proposal; not approved for classroom use.

This proposal narrows AASHA's broad product direction to one teacher-reviewed chapter so the team can evaluate source accuracy, authoring effort, offline operation, and student learning before expanding scope.

## Proposed pilot slice

- **Learners:** One agreed grade and cohort; exact age range and cohort size are undecided.
- **Chapter candidate:** Class 6 Mathematics, *Knowing Numbers*, using the existing [Studio sample](../../samples/demo_chapters/Class6_Mathematics_KnowingNumbers.html) only as a starting point. Confirm that the sample aligns with a named textbook edition and obtain source-use permission before treating it as textbook-grounded.
- **Lesson:** One short, interactive chapter with one visual activity tied to a stated learning objective, a brief reasoning check, and an offline HTML artifact.
- **Review:** A qualified teacher checks source references, explanations, questions, answers, language, accessibility, and any reused resource before learner release.
- **Release boundary:** No automated mastery, reward, or impact claims. Any learning guidance is presented as a draft for teacher review.

## Success measures to agree before the pilot

The following are proposed measures, not established targets or results. Confirm the targets and collection method with the pilot partner before enrolling learners.

| Measure | Proposed target | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Source and teacher integrity | Every factual lesson claim and question is page-linked or labeled teacher-authored; teacher approval recorded before release; no unresolved critical content issue | Source map and signed review checklist |
| Mentor authoring effort | At least 30% less active preparation time than the same mentor's baseline, with no lower rubric score | Timed baseline and pilot preparation; same review rubric |
| Learning and reasoning | At least 15 percentage-point mean improvement on a short, chapter-aligned pre/post check; report sample size and uncertainty | Paired pre/post responses scored with a pre-agreed rubric |
| Completion | At least 70% of enrolled learners complete the chapter | Local completion counts and documented denominator |
| Offline operation | All agreed core lesson steps work on the selected device/browser in airplane mode, without remote requests or blocking errors | Device/browser checklist, network inspection, and issue log |
| Privacy | No student names in lesson telemetry; consent and retention rules approved by the partner; sync disabled unless separately approved | Data inventory and partner-approved consent/retention record |

## Decisions still open

- Pilot partner, teacher reviewer, school/NGO, and approval owner
- Textbook board, edition, chapter pages, and source-use permission
- Grade, learner cohort, language mode, device/browser, and accessibility needs
- Whether the existing *Knowing Numbers* sample is accurate and suitable, or whether another chapter is preferred
- Pre/post assessment and scoring rubric
- Whether proposed numeric targets are appropriate for the cohort
- Consent process, event collection, retention period, and any sync configuration
- Pilot dates, support plan, and stop conditions for content or privacy issues

## Gate before classroom use

Do not enroll learners until the chapter and source rights are confirmed, a teacher approves the artifact, offline behavior is checked on agreed devices, assessment and privacy procedures are approved by the partner, and the success measures are agreed in writing. This document is a proposal; it does not certify the sample or authorize a pilot.
